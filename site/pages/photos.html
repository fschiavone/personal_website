I enjoy taking photos of concerts, trips, nature and, of course, stars and telescopes. My gear consists in a trusty Canon EOS 600D which I have owned for more than 10 years and various relatively inexpensive lenses, of which my most used lately are the Canon EF-S 24mm f/2.8 STM "pancake" (when I really want to be travelling light) and the Sigma 17-50mm f/2.8 EX DC OS HSM (which is basically a higher-quality, faster, and heavier version of your standard 18-55mm kit lens). My most recent photos can be found on my dedicated Instagram profile.
